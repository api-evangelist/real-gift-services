# RealGifts (Real Gift Services)

> Gifting-as-a-Service (GaaS) and G-Commerce platform, operating since 2008. Platforms and apps embed a turnkey gift shop, reward store, and wishlist in minutes, sell from a catalog of 1M+ gifts across hundreds of brands, and earn commissions on every order while RealGifts handles payments, shipping, redemption, and recipient-privacy-preserving delivery.
